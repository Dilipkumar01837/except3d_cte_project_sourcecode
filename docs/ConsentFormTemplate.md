## Purpose

You are invited to take part in a research study comparing a gamified programming platform with traditional coding exercises. The study examines motivation, learning, problem-solving time, retention, hints, and optional real-time competition.

## Procedures

If you agree, you will complete a baseline survey and assessment, use the assigned learning experience for approximately four weeks, complete a post-assessment, and complete a delayed assessment 2-4 weeks later. You may be invited to an optional interview. The platform may record assessment attempts, challenge timing, attempts, errors, hints, duel participation, and login/activity events when you explicitly opt in to telemetry.

## Risks and benefits

Risks are minimal but may include fatigue, frustration, or discomfort with comparative performance. You may skip any survey item or stop participation. You may not receive a direct personal benefit. The study may help improve programming-learning tools.

## Privacy

Research exports use a study ID or salted pseudonymous identifier. The research dataset will not contain your name, email address, IP address, or source code. The account-to-study-ID key is stored separately with restricted access. Results will be reported in aggregate. Data will be retained until [date/policy] and then deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without penalty and without losing normal platform access. To withdraw or request deletion, contact [research contact]. Turning off telemetry stops future telemetry collection; follow the study's withdrawal instructions to request deletion of study records.
